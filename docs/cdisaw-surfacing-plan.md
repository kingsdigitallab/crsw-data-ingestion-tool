## 10. If something goes wrong

- A deposit refused by the promoter stays in staging with the reason in the audit trail. Fix the cause, remove the refused one with `promoter remove --staging <id> --confirm <id>`, and re-stage that source with `cdisaw-parquet deposit --deposit --only <slug>`. (`refresh --only` takes script names, not slugs; the bulk CLI cannot find cdisaw's files on its own.) A re-run makes a new deposit and uploads every member of that dataset again; nothing is skipped in staging. The promoter merges it with what is already in place.
- A dataset that failed mid-upload leaves an unfinished deposit in staging (status open). The promoter skips it, but it counts against your staging quota. The `FAILED` line, the error in `data/deposit/staged-<time>.json` and the "To finish" hint all name its id and the `promoter remove --staging <id> --confirm <id>` that clears it; do that before the `--only` re-run.
- `remove --staging` on a finished deposit only works when the promoter refuses it for its own content (a stale copy that fails the re-hash, a wrong size, a missing object or record, an upload that died after the record was written). A refusal caused by the VM (`PROMOTER_AUTHORISED` without `centre`, a size cap, a vocabulary fetched without the providers) is reported and the deposit kept: fix the setting and run the promoter again rather than re-uploading.
- Ctrl-C during the upload is handled like a dropped VPN: the interrupted dataset and the ones not yet attempted are listed as failed, the ids so far are saved, and the `--only` printed covers the rest. Re-running the whole `deposit --deposit` is also safe: a dataset that already has a complete deposit waiting in staging is skipped, not staged twice. A mistyped `--only` is an error, not an empty success.
- A wrong record promoted: `promoter recategorise --changes` with `set` fixes descriptive fields in place; a wrong identifier or sensitivity needs `promoter remove --dataset <id> --confirm <id>` and a re-stage.
- The explorer misbehaving on the new path: `CDISAW_SOURCE` back to the test prefix, restart. The test prefix is untouched until section 9.
